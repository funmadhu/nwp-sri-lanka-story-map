# Story Structure: NWP Government Offices Story Map

## Narrative Framework

This story map tells the geographic story of government infrastructure in Sri Lanka's North West Province through five narrative beats, using maps, data, and visual storytelling.

---

## Scene 1: Introduction - Geographic Overview

### Narrative
"The North West Province of Sri Lanka encompasses Kurunegala and Puttalam districts, spanning approximately 8,000+ square kilometers. Across this diverse landscape—from coastal wetlands to inland agriculture—lies a network of government offices serving the local population."

### Map Elements
- **Base Layer**: Satellite imagery showing NWP geography
- **Boundaries**: District and provincial boundaries highlighted
- **Zoom Level**: Regional view (1:500,000 scale)
- **Focus Area**: Full NWP coverage

### Data Displayed
- Total area of NWP
- District boundaries
- Major towns and cities
- Geographic features (rivers, coast, etc.)

### Interactive Elements
- Allow user to explore satellite imagery
- Click to zoom into specific areas
- Toggle administrative boundaries on/off

---

## Scene 2: Government Infrastructure - Office Locations

### Narrative
"The province is served by 33 local government offices, including Pradeshiya Sabhas (village councils), Municipal councils, and District administrative centers. These offices form the backbone of local governance and public service delivery."

### Map Elements
- **Base Layer**: Satellite imagery (slightly less prominent)
- **Administrative Boundaries**: Districts and local councils
- **Main Layer**: All 33 government offices displayed as symbols
- **Zoom Level**: Medium view (1:250,000 scale)
- **Symbol Coding**:
  - Blue circle: Pradeshiya Sabha (primary level)
  - Red square: Municipal council
  - Green triangle: District secretariat

### Data Displayed
- All 33 office locations
- Office type classification
- Name labels (hoverable)
- Contact information (in identify results)

### Interactive Elements
- Hover over office to see name and type
- Click to view full details (name, address, phone, email)
- Zoom to individual office
- Show/hide office types via layer visibility

---

## Scene 3: Office Types & Distribution

### Narrative
"The local government system operates through three main tiers. Pradeshiya Sabhas represent the foundation, managing village-level administration. Municipal councils govern urban areas with greater resources. District secretariats coordinate provincial governance."

### Map Elements
- **Main Layer**: Government offices, color-coded by type
- **Statistics Panel**: Count of each office type
- **Zoom Level**: Medium-high (1:150,000 scale)
- **Highlighting**: Show offices by type

### Data Displayed
- **Pradeshiya Sabhas**: [X] offices, [%] of total
- **Municipal Councils**: [X] offices, [%] of total
- **District Secretariats**: [X] offices, [%] of total
- Geographic distribution by type

### Interactive Elements
- Toggle each office type on/off
- Filter to show single type
- View statistics in legend
- Compare spatial distribution

---

## Scene 4: Service Coverage & Accessibility

### Narrative
"These offices are distributed across diverse terrain, from coastal lowlands to inland plateau areas. Accessibility varies—some offices are centrally located and easily reached, while others serve remote communities and face geographic challenges."

### Map Elements
- **Base Layer**: Satellite imagery (full resolution)
- **Administrative Boundaries**: Pradeshiya Sabha divisions (each office's jurisdiction)
- **Offices**: Point locations
- **Optional**: Roads network, travel time isochrones
- **Zoom Level**: High (1:50,000 to 1:100,000 scale)
- **Color Coding**: Jurisdictional areas

### Data Displayed
- Service coverage area for each office
- Population served by each office
- Geographic characteristics (terrain, access)
- Distance between offices
- Travel time/accessibility metrics

### Interactive Elements
- Click office to highlight its service area
- Show distance to nearest office
- Identify accessibility challenges
- View population density in coverage area
- Compare urban vs. rural coverage patterns

## Design Principles

### Visual Hierarchy
1. **Satellite imagery** provides geographic context
2. **Administrative boundaries** define jurisdictions
3. **Government offices** are the focal point
4. **Labels and legends** support understanding

### Color Coding
- **Office Types**:
  - Pradeshiya Sabha: Blue (foundational level)
  - Municipal: Red (urban centers)
  - District: Green (coordination)
- **Boundaries**: Subtle lines or light fill
- **Satellite**: Natural colors (no modification)

### Typography
- **Title**: Large, clear, descriptive
- **Labels**: Medium size, readable
- **Legend**: Compact, well-organized
- **Attribution**: Small, visible

### Interactions
- **Identify**: Click any feature to learn more
- **Zoom**: Focus on areas of interest
- **Filter**: Show/hide specific office types
- **Export**: Save maps and data

## Narrative Flow (User Journey)

### Typical Exploration Path

1. **Start**: View full NWP overview
   - Understand geographic context
   - See provincial boundaries

2. **Explore**: Examine office locations
   - Identify where offices are located
   - Understand distribution pattern

3. **Discover**: Compare office types
   - See distribution of Pradeshiya Sabhas vs. Municipal councils
   - Understand governance structure

4. **Analyze**: Examine service coverage
   - See jurisdiction areas
   - Understand accessibility patterns

5. **Conclude**: Synthesize findings
   - Appreciate scope of local government network
   - Consider implications for governance

## Key Messages

1. **Comprehensive Coverage**: 33 offices serve the entire NWP population
2. **Hierarchical Structure**: Three-tier system from village to district level
3. **Geographic Challenges**: Accessibility varies with terrain and development
4. **Essential Infrastructure**: These offices are critical for public service delivery
5. **Opportunity for Improvement**: Data supports analysis for better service planning
